# 金融业务知识平台原型 / Financial workflow knowledge prototype

**性质 / Nature:** 个人学习与产品验证作品，使用完全虚构的基金定投场景。不是客户系统，也不提供投资建议。\
**Nature:** An independent product and learning prototype based on a fully fictional recurring fund investment workflow. It is not a client system or investment advice.

## 要解决的问题 / Problem

在长期迭代的业务系统中，需求、业务规则、设计、实现和测试经常分散。这个原型探索如何把它们连接成有来源、有版本、可核对的工程上下文。\
In an evolving business system, requirements, business rules, design, implementation, and tests can become disconnected. This prototype explores how to connect them into versioned, source-linked context.

## 我的工作 / My contribution

- 定义虚构基金定投流程的用户场景、业务规则、需求、验收标准和分阶段范围。\
  Defined fictional user scenarios, business rules, requirements, acceptance criteria, and staged scope.
- 将需求关联到原型页面、数据结构、接口和测试，记录假设与已验证内容。\
  Linked requirements to prototype screens, data structures, interfaces, and tests, while recording assumptions and validation state.
- 借助 AI 工具完成部分实现与文档整理，审查业务逻辑和验证结果。\
  Used AI tools to assist implementation and documentation, then reviewed business logic and validation results.

| 阶段 / Stage | 已完成的范围 / Completed scope |
| --- | --- |
