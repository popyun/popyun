# 企业员工储蓄系统升级 / Employee savings system upgrade

> 脱敏职业案例。业务分类为通用概括；不包含客户、企业、员工或交易数据，也不附雇主原始文档。\
> An anonymized account using general workflow categories. No client, employer, employee, transaction, or original internal documents are included.

## 背景与问题 / Context

既有员工储蓄系统的升级涉及企业与员工管理、契约、自动买入、卖出、持有、报表和统计等相互关联的流程。需求工作需要让业务规则、功能边界与开发处理保持一致。\
Upgrading an existing employee savings system involved connected workflows for organisation and employee administration, agreements, purchases, sales, holdings, reporting, and statistics. Requirements had to keep business rules and implementation boundaries aligned.

## 我的职责与关键行动 / My role and actions

- 梳理相关业务场景和流程，形成需求与功能设计文档。\
  I mapped relevant scenarios and workflows into requirements and functional design documents.
- 针对功能重构中的业务处理和数据核对，与开发人员讨论方案及边界。\
  I worked with engineers to clarify business handling and data checks during the functional redesign.
- 衔接需求、开发和测试，推动问题澄清与方案落实。\
  I connected requirements, engineering, and testing to resolve questions and carry the design into implementation.

## 结果与边界 / Outcome and scope

形成了可供开发与测试使用的需求及设计说明，并参与既有系统的功能重构。本案例强调我承担的需求和设计工作；不声称由我独立负责整个产品规划或系统上线。\
The requirements and designs supported engineering and testing, and I contributed to the redesign of the existing system. This account describes my requirements and design role; it does not claim ownership of the entire product strategy or release.

## 工作方法 / What this illustrates

把跨环节流程拆成明确的功能、规则与核对点，让业务方和工程方能够用同一套说明讨论实现。\
Break a connected workflow into clear functions, rules, and checks so business and engineering stakeholders can work from the same explanation.
