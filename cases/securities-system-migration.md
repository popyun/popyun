# 证券系统迁移 / Securities system migration

> 脱敏职业案例。仅描述我承担的模块职责，不披露客户、内部架构、迁移数据或配置。\
> An anonymized account of my module-level role. Client identity, internal architecture, migration data, and configuration are excluded.

## 背景与问题 / Context

既有证券系统的迁移涉及多个业务模块与团队。除技术环境变化外，还需要辨认模块依赖、业务数据范围及迁移后的验证要求。\
Migrating an established securities system involved several business modules and teams. The work required clarity on dependencies, relevant business data, and post-migration checks.

## 我的职责与关键行动 / My role and actions

- 作为负责模块的交付负责人，组织迁移准备、业务数据梳理和需迁移数据的筛选。\
  As the delivery lead for my modules, I organized preparation, reviewed business data, and defined the data selection required for migration.
- 协调模块成员及其他负责人，跟进依赖、任务进度和实施问题。\
  I coordinated the module team and other leads, tracking dependencies, progress, and implementation issues.
- 将本模块的业务核对纳入整体迁移安排，支持迁移实施。\
  I aligned module-level business checks with the wider migration plan and supported the implementation.

## 结果与边界 / Outcome and scope

我负责的模块完成迁移相关工作并支持整体实施。这里不把整个系统的迁移成果归于个人，也不公开服务器数量、团队规模或数据量。\
The modules under my responsibility completed their migration work and supported the wider implementation. This account does not attribute the whole system's outcome to one person or disclose infrastructure, staffing, or data volumes.

## 工作方法 / What this illustrates

迁移项目中的项目管理需要同时照顾技术步骤与业务连续性：先定义本模块的边界和核对点，再与其他模块同步依赖和节奏。\
Migration delivery requires attention to both technical steps and business continuity: define module boundaries and checks, then synchronize dependencies and timing across teams.
