> An anonymized account of my own responsibilities, not an official project or employer statement. Client identities, internal rule details, and production data are omitted.

## 背景与问题 / Context

证券业务规则变化会同时影响业务流程、系统处理和测试口径。项目需要在既有系统中识别受影响的功能，形成可供开发与测试共同使用的需求和验收依据。\
A change in securities business rules affected existing workflows, system behavior, and test expectations. The team needed a shared implementation and acceptance basis.

## 我的职责与关键行动 / My role and actions

- 负责相关业务模块的需求分析与交付组织，梳理业务流程及受影响功能，形成需求文档并向开发团队解释处理要求。\
  I led requirements analysis and delivery for the modules in my scope, mapped affected workflows, documented requirements, and clarified expected behavior with engineers.
- 安排模块任务与进度，协调其他模块负责人处理依赖和变化，并跟踪开发与测试问题。\
  I planned work and tracked progress, coordinated cross-module dependencies and changes, and followed through on development and test issues.
- 牵头制定集成测试计划与用例、组织测试，并参与客户验收。\
  I led integration-test planning and test cases, organized testing, and supported client acceptance.

## 结果与边界 / Outcome and scope

我负责的模块按计划完成交付，并完成对应的集成测试工作。这是模块范围内的交付结果；整体项目由多团队共同完成。公开版本暂不使用团队规模、工期或其他数字，待逐项核准。\
The modules under my responsibility were delivered as planned, with their integration-testing work completed. The wider programme was a multi-team effort. Team size, dates, and other figures are omitted pending individual approval.

## 工作方法 / What this illustrates

从业务规则出发，明确影响范围和可验收行为，再把需求、开发、测试和跨模块协同接成同一条交付链。\
Start with the business rule, define the affected scope and testable behavior, then connect requirements, engineering, testing, and cross-module coordination into one delivery path.
